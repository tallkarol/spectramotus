A wave when you say hello. A thumbs-up in return. Subtle movement while it waits. Spectramotus is a way to give a single portrait a little life with the help of some AI magic.

Your gesture.
Their response.

The idea is simple: wave or give a thumbs-up, and the portrait responds. Between gestures, it settles into quiet movement.

A few things to know.

Can I try it now?

The studio is currently invite-only. If you’ve been invited, sign in with your email link. New accounts aren’t created automatically.

What’s available in this preview?

You can upload a photo, choose Motus, preview a spectrum, and download its pack. The team’s example uses existing reaction clips. Other photos currently use labelled still-photo placeholders. New video generation and payments are not enabled yet.

Do I need a physical frame?

No. You can try the player on your computer first. An offline Raspberry Pi starter package exists; testing on the final frame hardware is still ahead.
